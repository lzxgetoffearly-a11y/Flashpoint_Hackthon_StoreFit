## Survey (Flashpoint Surveys)
Goal: what first-time owners worry about when choosing and fitting out a space.
- Synthetic panel (ConnectedSynth): e.g. "50 people planning to open a small cafe or food shop in NYC within 12 months". Label all results **SYNTHETIC**.
- Real respondents (recommended by Flashpoint for calibration): public link to classmates / founder groups, or a Prolific quote screened for small-business owners. Label **REAL**. Do not submit self-generated answers.
- Compare synthetic vs real: where they agree / disagree. This comparison is the evidence, plus its limits.

Draft questions:
1. Have you opened, or do you plan to open, a food/retail business? (screen)
2. Top 3 factors when choosing a location (rank)
3. Biggest fear about signing a lease (open)
4. Did/would you use any data tool for site selection? Which, and why not?
5. How did/would you plan the interior layout? (designer / contractor / yourself)
6. Most costly surprise after signing (open)
7. Would a free tool showing competitors, transit and a 3D fit before signing change your decision? (1–5)
8. What would make you trust its score? (open)
