# StoreFit demo: narration for storefit-agent-demo.mp4 (2:17)

**0:00 Intro**
"StoreFit's research agent takes one objective: what cafe should open at 2905 Broadway. In this run the
surveys, emails and payments were real. The answers were not: five AI-generated
persona responses per round, submitted by our AI assistant. No independent human answered."

**0:07 Agent log replay (waits shortened)**
"It writes down its hypotheses and how sure it is. It checks what the room can hold. It notices that the
demographics data only covers all of Manhattan, and doesn't rely on it. Then it writes, publishes and sends
its own survey through the Flashpoint MCP, paying per call in USDC."

**About 0:35**
"Five AI-generated test responses split two, two, one across the layouts. Flashpoint itself flagged them as synthetic, and
we label them that way. The agent updates its confidence and writes a second survey aimed at that tie."

**About 0:55**
"When a send timed out, it asked for the status, with permission to send if needed, and Flashpoint reported the
seven invitations as sent. A team member ended some of the waits by hand. In the second round the test responses went
three to two for the bigger room, so it chose Max seating, twenty-one seats, and said the margin was one vote."

**1:26 Live site**
"StoreFit ranks vacant storefronts on public data. Our test site, 2905 Broadway, is not the top-ranked one.
Each layout the agent compares is a checked 3D plan: fourteen seats with a sofa, nine for grab-and-go,
twenty-one for maximum seating."

**2:10 Close**
"This shows the loop working. It does not show demand: that needs independent human respondents. And all
three layouts fail the one-exit check, which an architect would need to resolve."

## Don't say
- "Five customers told us", "validated with users", or any percentage from this run.
- "The agent chose Max seating" without adding "on AI-generated test responses".
- "As the organisers suggested", unless they did.

## Source of the answers
5 AI-generated test-persona responses per round, submitted by the AI assistant through the public survey link at
the team's direction. 0 independent human respondents.
